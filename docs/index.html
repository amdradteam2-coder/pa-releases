PA

A Windows 11 tray assistant that does small jobs on your PC, and lets you undo every one of them.

Download for Windows
  • Sort a folder, digest a folder into one page, or summarize a PDF or Word document.
  • See what's waiting in Gmail, Outlook, or GitHub as a short note on your PC.
  • Type, hold the mic to talk (speech is turned into text on your PC), or drop a file on the panel.
  • Every job shows what it will read and write before it starts. File jobs run in a sandbox with no network. Undo moves what PA made to the Recycle Bin.

No account, no servers, no analytics. Privacy policy · All releases · Questions and problems

Requires Windows 11 24H2 or later. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to choose More info, then Run anyway.
