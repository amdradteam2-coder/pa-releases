PA privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

PA is a Windows app that runs on your PC. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The people who make PA never receive your files, your mail, or anything about how you use the app.

What stays on your PC

  • Files you hand to PA. When you sort, digest, or summarize, PA reads only the folder or file named in the request you approve, and writes the result next to it. File jobs run in a Windows sandbox with the network turned off.
  • Your voice. When you hold the mic button, the recording is turned into text on your PC and then discarded. The recording is never saved or sent anywhere; the words it heard show in the conversation like typed ones. PA never listens unless you press the button.
  • Every day. A job you choose to repeat runs at the same time each day on your PC, reading only what that job read the first time. Settings › Every day lists them and stops any of them.
  • The window behind PA. When you open PA, it notes the title of the window you were using (and the folder, if it's File Explorer) so it can offer to help with it. You can block windows by phrase in Settings. PA never takes screenshots.
  • Job history, the conversation, settings, and the activity log are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PA, separately for Work and Personal. New chat clears the conversation. Notes are kept in Documents\PA.
  • What you allowed. When you choose Always allow, PA remembers that kind of job (or that one connected account) for that profile, on your PC, so it doesn't ask again. Settings › Permissions lists them and removes any of them; disconnecting an account removes its permission.
  • The optional local model runs on your PC through Foundry Local. Text it summarizes does not leave the PC.

Google data (Gmail and Google Calendar)

If you connect Gmail, PA asks Google for two read-only permissions:

  • gmail.metadata: the sender, subject, and date of your unread messages. This permission cannot read the text of any message or its attachments.
  • calendar.events.readonly: the title and time of today's events.

PA uses this information for one purpose: writing a short "what's waiting" note in Documents\PA on your PC when you ask for it. PA never sends, deletes, moves, labels, or changes anything in your Google account.

The data goes directly from Google to your PC. It is not sent to the makers of PA or to anyone else, not shared or sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train or improve any AI or machine-learning model. It is not given to the local model either.

PA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To stay signed in, PA keeps the sign-in (a refresh token) in Windows Credential Manager on your PC, separately for the Work and Personal profiles. Settings › Connected › Disconnect deletes it and asks Google to revoke it. Uninstalling PA does the same. You can also remove PA's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Outlook and GitHub

PA reads unread mail subjects and today's calendar through the Outlook app already signed in on your PC, and your open pull requests and issues through the GitHub CLI you signed in to. Both are read-only, the results go into a note on your PC, and PA does not store either sign-in.

Updates

PA checks this project's GitHub releases for updates. That request is an ordinary download from GitHub and carries nothing about you or your files.

Deleting your data

Everything PA keeps is on your PC. Undo moves a result to the Recycle Bin. Deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\PA and Documents\PA removes everything else.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue.
